---
title: Silbro's Journal, III
author: Silbro
---

Autumn, and I am old.

Stone has written at last. Three lines. The boy is gone from the forge; he does not say where, and I do not ask. I have had his silence long enough to read it.

---

The oldest wood I know is two days north. It has been calling me since spring, the way the willow did, only slower, and much deeper.

I think it is my turn to be given to something.

---

I leave the willow's ring on the sill, for whoever needs it. Not for whoever wants it. It will know the difference; it always has.

Tell Stone I was right about waiting. Tell him kindly.
