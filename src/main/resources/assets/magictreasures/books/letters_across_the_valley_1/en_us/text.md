---
title: Letters Across the Valley, I
author: Sal'andaar and Silbro
---

Friend,

The willow you laughed at has given me a second ring. The pearl woman's runner brings it, so you may laugh at it in person.

Keep it from the forge. It dislikes fire.

\- Silbro

---

Silbro,

Ring arrived. Laughed. Put it on the anvil and hit it. Hammer bounced.

What did you do to it?

\- S. Stone

---

Nothing. That is the whole of it. I waited, and the tree did the rest.

You hit things until they agree with you. I wait until they agree with me. We both get rings.

\- Silbro

---

Silbro,

Waiting is not a craft.

Sent you a ward for your door. Keeps out fever, thieves and wolves. Does not keep out squirrels. Did not try.

\- S. Stone

---

The squirrels thank you. They are living in it.

It is a fine ward, my friend, the finest closed thing I have ever seen. I only wish you would leave something open now and then.

\- Silbro

---

Silbro,

Open things leak.

Come down for the midsummer fair. Bring the ring. I want to hit it again.

\- S.
