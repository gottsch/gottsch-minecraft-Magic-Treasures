---
title: The Runesmith's Ledger
author: Sal'andaar
---

Ledger of S. Stone, runesmith.

Three horseshoes, a mended plough. 4 copper.

A ward set in a farmer's ring, against fevers. 1 silver. He paid in eggs. Wrote it down as a silver anyway.

---

Iron ring, no stone, for the Dunmore farmer. Swears it brings him luck.

Charged him for the iron. Not for the luck.

![](preset:magictreasures:peasants_fortune)

---

Old hammer split. Cut my mark into the face of the new one, so every piece I strike carries it.

Folk call it the stonemark now. Fine.

![](item:magictreasures:stonemark_hammer)

---

Took on two apprentices.

Bren: strong, slow, honest.

Maldritch: best hands I've had. Learns a weld in a day that took me a year.

Asks too much about the dead.

---

New cuff for my own wrist; sparks again. Closed a ward in it that sends a blow back to whoever struck it.

Bren tested it. Bren has a bruise.

![](preset:magictreasures:smiths_cuff)

---

Castle rings: rubies and sapphires, nothing else. A customer asked why.

Because.

![](preset:magictreasures:silver_castle_ring)

---

Silbro sent another ring that grew itself. Sent him back a horseshoe that didn't.

He'll know what I meant.

---

Copper amulet, topaz, against hexes. For the reeve's wife. 6 silver.

![](preset:magictreasures:amulet_of_defence)

---

Gold ring set with one of the pearl woman's white pearls. Mends slow, but mends.

She named it Angel's Ring. Sold three before I could argue.

![](preset:magictreasures:angels_ring)

---

Gold token, jadeite, for the village healer. She already wears the druid's elm one.

Said she wanted one that won't sprout.

![](preset:magictreasures:medics_token)

---

The coat is done. Took the winter. Spells slide off it like rain off oilcloth.

Tested it on myself. Then on Maldritch, at his asking. He laughed the whole time.

Should not have let him.

---

The Ward is done. Gold, a ruby, and the strongest tower I ever closed into a necklace.

Wears heavy. Meant to.

![](preset:magictreasures:salandaars_ward)

---

Maldritch gone three days. Came back with a bone charm that holds mana.

Bone doesn't hold mana. I know what does, and so does he.

We did not speak of it.

---

A ring for myself. Gold, a sapphire, and a ward that thickens the shadows when something swings at me.

Calling it Fortitude. Needed some.

![](preset:magictreasures:ring_of_fortitude)

---

The old anvil cracked. Thirty years under my hammer. Couldn't scrap it, so I cut a shield from it.

Takes a blow better than anything else I made.

![](item:magictreasures:anvil_shield)

---

Bren has left. Says he won't work beside "that one".

Orders are down. Folk in the market cross the street.

The ward for the reeve's door: no charge.
