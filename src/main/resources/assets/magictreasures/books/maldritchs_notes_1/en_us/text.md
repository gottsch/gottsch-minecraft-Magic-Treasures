title: Maldritch's Notes, I
author: Maldritch
---

Master Stone says magic is set, not made. A hammer closes it into metal like a weld.

I have watched him do it a hundred times and I still cannot see the moment it happens.

I will.

---

A summer with the druid. Silbro talks to willows and they listen. He let me hold a ring he grew. It was warm.

If a living thing can hold magic, it is already full of it. Why wait to be given?

---

Mother is sick. The healers' charms do nothing.

Silbro came himself and sat with her three days. He was kind. He was patient.

She died on the fourth.

---

He said some things cannot be mended, only let go.

I have decided he is wrong.

The gentle way is too slow.

---

A rabbit. Bone holds nothing; everyone knows that. But I closed a ward around the life as it left, the way Master Stone closes a weld, and the bone kept it.

It was warm, like the willow ring.

![](preset:magictreasures:maldritchs_first_amulet)

---

I have told no one. Master Stone would not understand. Silbro would understand too well.

I am not doing anything wrong. I am only learning faster.
