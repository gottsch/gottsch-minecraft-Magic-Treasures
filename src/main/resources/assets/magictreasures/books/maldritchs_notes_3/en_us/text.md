---
title: Maldritch's Notes, III
author: Maldritch
---

The village held more than I needed. I carry it in the belt, and the belt carries me.

Master Stone has put me out of the forge. He did not shout. I think I would have preferred it.

---

Bone outlasts flesh. So I have closed my life into my own bones, the way I closed the rabbit's, only slowly, and from the inside.

It did not hurt. Nothing hurts now.

---

I needed a blade that would never leave me, so I made one from what I had: a thighbone, and the long curve of a rib.

It drinks a little from every cut and gives it back to me.

![](item:magictreasures:bone_scythe)

---

The children in the next valley ran from me. I have made a mask, so that the living see a man when they look.

It is a good likeness. I used to look like that.

![](item:magictreasures:lichs_mask)

---

And a ring, to keep what I am. Blood-metal for the band, a ruby to hold it. While it holds, I cannot die.

It draws what it needs from whoever stands near. It never asks.

![](preset:magictreasures:ring_of_life_death)

---

I remember being afraid. I don't remember why.

Stone is coming. I can feel his wards closing, one by one, like doors down a long hall.

Let him come.
