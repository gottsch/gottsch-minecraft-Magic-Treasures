title: Silbro's Journal, I
author: Silbro
---

Spring.

The smiths in the valley laughed when I told them wood could hold magic. They are right, in their way. Dead wood cannot. Cut a branch and the magic runs out of it like sap.

---

So I did not cut the willow by the mill. I sat with it instead, and asked.

A tree answers slowly. It took the whole of that first summer before I felt it lean toward my hand.

---

Second summer.

A root has curled around my finger while I slept. I have not moved it. It is warm, which roots are not meant to be, and when I set a little healing into it, the healing stayed.

---

Third summer.

The ring is grown. It holds mana as well as any silver ring I have seen at the forge, and when I cracked it on a stone it mended itself, once.

I do not think it will do that twice.

---

Autumn.

The ring has made me greedy. My walking staff split in the first frost, and instead of cutting another I asked a root by the stream to grow straight for me.

It is thinking about it.

---

I showed it to Sal'andaar. He turned it over for a long time and said only, "Show me how."

I told him the truth: you cannot make it. You can only wait to be given it.

He did not like that answer.

---

It is a little pleased with itself, I think.

So am I.

\- S.
