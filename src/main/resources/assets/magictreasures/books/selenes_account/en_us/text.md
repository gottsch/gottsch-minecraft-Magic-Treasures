title: Selene's Account
author: Selene Tidewalker
---

They will tell you there were three: the runesmith, the druid, and the one we don't name in harbours.

There were four. I sold the other three their pearls, and I am the one still here to write it down.

---

Sal'andaar thought magic was a thing you hammered shut. Silbro thought it was a thing you were given.

They argued for forty years and were both right, which neither of them could stand.

---

Pearls are grown, you know. An oyster takes a grain of grit, a wound, and wraps it in light, year after year.

Silbro understood at once. Sal'andaar asked what the oyster charged.

---

My own first pearls were small and badly shaped, and they were the best I ever found, because I found them.

I set them in a circlet of silver, and I have worn it every day since.

![](item:magictreasures:pearl_circlet)

---

I made bands for my couriers, so they could run the coast roads by night and see the ruts before they fell in them.

Half the letters between the runesmith and the druid went by my runners. I read none of them. Mostly.

![](preset:magictreasures:journeyman_bands)

---

And the boy. Maldritch. Clever hands, frightened eyes. He asked me once how long a pearl lives.

I told him: longer than the oyster.

I have wondered since if that was the worst thing I ever said.

---

I have kept their papers, what I could find. Read them as you find them.

You will not find them all. I have seen to that.

\- S. T.
