---
title: A Warning, Posted in the Market
author: The Reeve
---

BY ORDER OF THE REEVE

The mill village on the east road stands empty. No sickness was found there, and no raiders seen. Its folk lie in their beds as if asleep, and they are cold.

---

Buy no charms of bone. Trade with no pale young smith who sells them, nor give him lodging. If he is seen, send word to the reeve, and to Master Stone at the forge.

Do not hold what he offers. It is warm.
