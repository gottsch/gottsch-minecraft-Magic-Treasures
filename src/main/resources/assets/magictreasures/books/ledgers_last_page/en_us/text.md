Last page.

The boy was mine to teach. He is mine to finish.

Forty years of wards, and the best one was never for a door. Closed it into the Orb: every weld I know, folded in on itself.

---

Made a helm last night from the last of the good steel. One night, start to finish.

Didn't sleep anyway.

![](item:magictreasures:smiths_helm)

---

Found him below the old chapel, among his bones. He didn't fight. Asked if it would hurt. Told him I didn't know.

Shut the crypt. The ward holds while the Orb is whole, and nobody holds the Orb but me.

---

Can't leave it where his kind could find it. Taking it north, past where Silbro went. Somewhere old enough that nothing digs.
