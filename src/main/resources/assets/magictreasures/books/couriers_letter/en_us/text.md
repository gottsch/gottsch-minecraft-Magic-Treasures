---
title: A Courier's Letter
author: Tam, a courier
---

Dear Mam,

I am a courier now, for the pearl lady up the coast. She gave me silver bands that let me see at night, so I run the coast road in the dark like a fox with lanterns for eyes.

![](preset:magictreasures:journeyman_bands)

---

Last night I carried a letter from the druid to the runesmith. It was eleven pages. The runesmith's answer was one line. I waited an hour for it.

I am paid by the letter, not the page. I have raised this with her.

---

A bat flew into my face at the river ford, and I saw it coming, which was worse.

I am eating well. Do not worry. Tell Pip he cannot have my room.

Your loving son,\
Tam
