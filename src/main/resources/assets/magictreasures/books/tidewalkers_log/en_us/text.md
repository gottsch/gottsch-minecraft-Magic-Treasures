---
title: The Tidewalker's Log
author: Selene Tidewalker
---

Spring tide, full moon.

Pearls brought up: eleven. Good ones: two. The oysters keep their own counsel.

Noted again: the dive is easier under a full moon. The sea gives back what you spend in it.

![](item:magictreasures:white_pearl)

---

Set a topaz in silver for the deep beds. Wearing it, I breathe the sea as easily as the air above it.

The couriers want one each. They are not getting one.

![](preset:magictreasures:aqua_ring)

---

Neap tide.

Ran out of light on the coast road and put the cart in a ditch. Lost a wheel and a temper.

Must make the couriers something better than lanterns.

---

New moon.

The bands are done: plain silver, a sliver of pearl set inside each, against the skin. Worn at night, the road shows grey instead of black.

The couriers call them journeyman's bands. I let them.

![](preset:magictreasures:journeyman_bands)

---

Spring tide again.

Stone asks why my pieces cost less to work by night. I told him the moon pulls the sea, the sea pulls the pearl, and the pearl pulls whatever is set around it.

He wrote that down.

---

Full moon. Raiders off the point.

Stone forged me a glaive with a moon-silver edge. It answers the moon the way the tide does, and it has never once needed sharpening.

![](item:magictreasures:moonglaive)

---

A black pearl from the deepest bed, warm as a coal. I set it in gold.

The lighthouse caught fire that autumn. I walked in for the keeper's daughter and walked out again. The couriers call it the Eye of the Phoenix now.

![](preset:magictreasures:eye_of_the_phoenix)

---

High water.

Swam out past the bar at dusk to check the beds, and came back with more in me than I took out. I am forty years older than my couriers, and I swim the way they run.

I have stopped asking why.
