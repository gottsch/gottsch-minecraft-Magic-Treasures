---
title: Maldritch's Notes, II
author: Maldritch
---

The rabbit bone held nine days, then went cold. A ward closed at the moment of death keeps what is left, and what is left is very little.

Take it before the end, not at it.

---

A goat, sedated. I drew from it while it lived, into a ward of thigh bone. The bone held four times what the rabbit gave, and the goat lived a day after.

Living things refill. Silbro was right about that much.

---

Bone is the right vessel. It was alive once; it remembers being full, and it wants to be again. Silver only holds. Bone pulls.

Master Stone would call that a leak. It is a well.

---

The belt is finished: small skulls on a band of iron, each one a ward. Worn, it feeds a ring as a spring feeds a pond.

I wore it to the forge. Master Stone did not look at it. Bren did.

![](item:magictreasures:skull_belt)

---

Animals are slow. Each holds a little and takes a season to fill again, and the woods near the forge are going quiet.

I need a vessel that holds more, and lives in numbers.

---

The mill on the east road has forty souls.

I have been counting.
