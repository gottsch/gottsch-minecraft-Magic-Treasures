---
title: Selene's Last Page
author: Selene Tidewalker
---

I am the last. A pearl does not age while the sea keeps it, and the sea has kept me longer than I asked.

Silbro is a wood now. Stone went north with his Orb and did not come back. The boy is where Stone left him.

---

I gathered what they wrote. Most of it is harmless: a druid's seasons, a smith's accounts, the letters of two stubborn old friends.

The boy's notes are not. Read in order, they are a recipe.

---

So I have scattered them, one book to a chest, from here to the far coast.

If you ever hold them all, burn the boy's. Keep the letters. They were the best of us.

\- S. T.
