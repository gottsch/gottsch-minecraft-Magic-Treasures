---
title: Silbro's Journal, II
author: Silbro
---

Autumn.

Hale the woodcutter came up the hill with his arm in a sling. Thirty years of the axe, and the elbow gave out before he did.

I did not mend the arm. I asked the oak he cuts to help him.

---

The bracers took the winter: oak bark, still on the tree when I began, grown around a band of willow.

He swings as he did at twenty. He says he can hear the oak in them when he works. I expect he can.

![](preset:magictreasures:strongmans_bracers)

---

Winter, and a hard one. The mill village ran out of flour before midwinter.

I gave an acorn from my oldest oak to every family that came up the hill. Planted, they will feed the village in a few years. Worn on a cord, they keep the hunger off until then.

![](preset:magictreasures:silbros_acorn)

---

The root by the stream has finished my staff: straight as a spear, and still alive at the foot.

It does not like to be leaned on. It likes to be swung.

![](item:magictreasures:rootstaff)

---

Spring.

The village healer asked for something she could carry. I gave her a token cut from a living elm, no bigger than a thumbnail.

It does not heal. It only reminds the hurt that they can.

---

The old beech by the ford shed a sheet of its bark in the spring storms. I wore it home, and it took to me.

By summer the beech had grown it back. A tree that gives twice deserves to be thanked twice, so I have.

![](item:magictreasures:barkskin_vest)

---

Summer.

Sal'andaar has sent me his best apprentice, "to learn patience." His name is Maldritch. Quick hands, quick eyes, and a sadness he wears like a cloak in August.

---

He listens to the willows better than I did at his age. I let him hold the ring. He held it a long while.

Then he asked me how it could be emptied. Not filled. Emptied.

---

I told him a ring is not a cup. What it holds was given, and given things are not taken back.

He nodded as if he understood. I do not think he believed me. He goes home tomorrow.

\- S.
