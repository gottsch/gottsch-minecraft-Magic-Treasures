```spotlight magictreasures:skull_belt
```

A **mana well**: it holds **250 mana** for your other jewelry. Wear it in the Curios belt slot, hold it in either hand, or (without Curios) keep it in your hotbar.

- When a spell costs more mana than its jewelry has left, the **rest comes from the belt**. The jewelry's own mana is always used first, so jewelry with 0 mana keeps casting while the belt has some.
- It has no spells, durability or gem of its own.
- **Recharge** it at [the anvil](entry:anvil#recharge) like jewelry: 2 recharges, each refilling it completely.
- Found in Rare and better chests. It's also a piece of [Maldritch's Remains](entry:sets#maldritch).
