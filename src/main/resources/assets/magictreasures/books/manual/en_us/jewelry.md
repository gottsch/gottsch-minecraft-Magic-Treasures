There are three types of jewelry: rings, necklaces and bracelets. Each piece is made of a material, comes in a style, and can have a gem. Together they set its uses, mana, repairs, recharges and the highest spell level it can hold.

Set a gem and add a spell at an anvil. A piece holds one spell; some named pieces hold more.

## Crafting Jewelry {#crafting}

Craft a ring, necklace or bracelet from its recipe scroll and four of a material: iron, copper, silver or gold ingots, or bones.

Crafted jewelry is the regular style, with no gem. Wood jewelry, Great and Lord's jewelry, and jewelry with a gem already set are found as loot.

## Jewelry Styles {#jewelry_style}

Jewelry comes in three styles. Regular is the plain one, with no word in its name. The others multiply the material's uses and mana, and add to its level and repairs.
