Everything you do to jewelry after making it happens at an anvil: the **jewelry in the left slot**, the other item in the right. Each action costs **1 experience level** and uses up the item in the right slot.

## Add a Gem {#add_gem}

A gem in the right slot. The jewelry gains the gem's mana and recharges; its mana, durability, repairs and spells carry over. See [Gems](entry:gems).

Some jewelry is picky: Castle Rings only take a Ruby or Sapphire, and Hawk Rings and most [named jewelry](entry:named_jewelry) can't take a gem at all.

## Remove a Gem {#remove_gem}

```spotlight minecraft:shears
```

Shears in the right slot. {dark_red}The gem is destroyed{}, and so are the shears. Use this to swap a weak gem for a better one. To keep the gem instead, use [Jewelry Pliers](entry:crafting#pliers).

## Add a Spell {#add_spell}

A spell scroll in the right slot.

- The scroll's level must be **no higher than the jewelry's Spell Max. Level**.
- Jewelry can only take a spell **while it has none**.

A scroll's colour shows its level: {gold}yellow{} 1-2, {dark_green}green{} 3-4, {dark_blue}blue{} 5-6, {dark_red}red{} 7-8, {black}black{} 9 and up. See [Spells](entry:spells).

## Recharge {#recharge}

```spotlight magictreasures:recharge_scroll
```

An **Amethyst Shard**, **Emerald** or **Recharge Scroll** in the right slot refills the mana completely and uses 1 recharge.

The jewelry {dark_red}must have a gem{}: recharges come from gems. A [Skull Belt](entry:skull_belt) recharges the same way.

## Repair {#repair}

Any piece of Magic Treasures jewelry in the right slot (it's used up). Restores full durability and uses 1 repair.

Jewelry with no repairs left can't be repaired; some starts with none, like Regular Wood and Bone jewelry. Repair before durability reaches 0, because at 0 the jewelry breaks.
