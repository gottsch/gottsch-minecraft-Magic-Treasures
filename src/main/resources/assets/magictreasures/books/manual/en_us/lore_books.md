Fifteen books tell the story behind the [named jewelry](entry:named_jewelry) and the [sets](entry:sets): journals, ledgers, notes and letters by Silbro, Sal'andaar, Maldritch and Selene, and by a few people who knew them.

## Finding Them {#finding}

Lore books are chest loot, of the chest's rarity, and never more than one in a chest. Every book is Uncommon or rarer. See [Where to Find](entry:where_to_find#chests).

## Reading Them {#reading}

Use a book to open it. Turn pages with the arrows, the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, or the mouse wheel. Pictures show the jewelry the text is about; hover over one for its tooltip. Notes in brackets are the writer's asides. The book remembers your page until you close the game.

## The Books {#books}

- **Silbro's Journal**, I to III
- **The Runesmith's Ledger** and **The Ledger's Last Page** (Sal'andaar)
- **Letters Across the Valley**, I and II (Sal'andaar and Silbro)
- **Maldritch's Notes**, I to III
- **Selene's Account**, **The Tidewalker's Log** and **Selene's Last Page**
- **A Warning, Posted in the Market** and **A Courier's Letter**

## Advancements {#advancements}

Reading your first book gives **First Pages**, and reading all fifteen gives **Archivist**. Each writer also has a hidden advancement for reading all of their books.
