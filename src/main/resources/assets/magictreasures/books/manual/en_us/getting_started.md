From nothing to a piece of jewelry casting spells for you.

## 1. Get some jewelry {#get}

**Find it:** jewelry, gems and spell scrolls turn up in chests and drop from many mobs. See [Where to Find](entry:where_to_find).

**Craft it:** a Ring, Necklace or Bracelet Recipe scroll and 4 ingots (iron, copper, silver or gold) or 4 bones. See [Crafting](entry:crafting).

Hover over jewelry to see its stats. Hold **Shift** for the full tooltip.

## 2. Wear it {#wear}

- **Without Curios:** hold it or keep it in your hotbar. Only the first 4 jewelry items in your hotbar count.
- **With Curios:** put it in your ring, necklace or bracelet slot. Hotbar jewelry then no longer casts.

## 3. Give it a spell {#spell}

Crafted jewelry starts with no spells. Put it in the left slot of an anvil and a **spell scroll** in the right slot. The scroll's level must be no higher than the jewelry's **Spell Max. Level**, and the jewelry must have no spell yet. See [The Anvil](entry:anvil#add_spell) and [Spells](entry:spells).

## 4. Let it cast {#cast}

Spells cast by themselves: healing when you're hurt, shields and resistances when you're hit, attacks when hostile mobs are near, and buffs on a cooldown.

Every cast costs **mana** and **1 durability**.

- Mana {dark_red}doesn't refill on its own{}. When it runs out, the jewelry stops casting until you recharge it.
- At 0 durability the jewelry {dark_red}breaks and is gone{}. Repair it before then.

## 5. Make it stronger {#stronger}

- **Set a gem** for more mana, recharges and better spells. Jewelry without a gem can't be recharged. See [Gems](entry:gems).
- **Recharge** it with an Amethyst Shard, Emerald or Recharge Scroll.
- **Repair** it with another piece of Magic Treasures jewelry.

Each piece has only a few recharges and repairs, shown in its tooltip.

## Tips {#tips}

- **Great** and **Lord's** jewelry hold more and reach higher spell levels than Regular. See [Jewelry](entry:jewelry#jewelry_style).
- Materials matter: gold casts cheaper, bone hits harder, iron makes buffs last longer. See [Materials](entry:materials).
- Look out for [named jewelry](entry:named_jewelry): unique pieces that come with spells, and often never wear out.
