## Hawk Rings {#hawk_rings}

```spotlight preset:magictreasures:great_gold_hawk_ring
```

Far more mana than standard jewelry, and spells one level higher than their material allows. They {dark_red}can't take a gem{}, so they can't be recharged: once the mana is spent, it's gone. They come in wood, iron, copper, silver and gold, Regular, Great and Lord's.
