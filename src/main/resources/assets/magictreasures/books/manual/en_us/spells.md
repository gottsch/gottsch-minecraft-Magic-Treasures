Every spell with its base numbers. The jewelry's material and gem change cost, effect, duration, cooldown and range, so a real piece's tooltip can differ a little.

- **Level:** the jewelry's Spell Max. Level must be at least this to take the scroll. Add a spell at [the anvil](entry:anvil#add_spell).
- **+** after a name means several pieces with that spell all cast. Without it, only one spell of that kind casts at a time.
- Each cast costs its mana and 1 durability.

A scroll's colour shows its level: {gold}yellow{} 1-2, {dark_green}green{} 3-4, {dark_blue}blue{} 5-6, {dark_red}red{} 7-8, {black}black{} 9 and up.
