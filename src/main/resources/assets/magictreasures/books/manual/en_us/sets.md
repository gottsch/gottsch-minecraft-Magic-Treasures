Each of the four legendary jewelers left a set: their jewelry, plus a weapon and armor made to go with it. Wear enough pieces of one set and its bonuses switch on: at **2** pieces, at **4**, and with the **whole set**. Each tier adds to the ones below it.

- **Worn** means Curios slots (without Curios, the hotbar), both hands, and armor slots.
- A set piece's tooltip lights up the pieces you're wearing and the bonuses that are on.
- **Siphon (weapons):** a full-strength hit on a mob puts mana back into the worn jewelry that has the least.
- **Resonance (armor):** each worn armor piece of a set gives that set's jewelry spells 10% more effect.
- Set weapons and armor carry no spells. They drop from Uncommon and better chests, at most one per chest.

## Sal'andaar's Anvilwork {#salandaar}

```spotlight magictreasures:stonemark_hammer
The runesmith's set: wards, and work that lasts. Sal'andaar's Ward, Ring of Fortitude, Runesmith's Cuff, Stonemark Hammer, Runesmith's Helm and Anvil Shield.
```

- **2:** ward spells cost 10% less mana.
- **4:** ward spells recover 15% faster.
- **6, Tempered:** worn set jewelry is stamped, and its next anvil repair is free.

Ward spells are Magic Resistance, the mana shields, Reflection and the armor spells. The **Stonemark Hammer** does 11 damage, slowly (siphon 2).

## Silbro's Grove {#silbro}

```spotlight magictreasures:rootstaff
The naturalist's set: living wood, and healing. Silbro's Ring of Vitality, Strongman's Bracers, Silbro's Acorn, Rootstaff and Barkskin Vest.
```

- **2:** healing spells heal 15% more, and 25% more often.
- **4:** standing on grass, moss or leaves slowly heals you.
- **5, Take Root:** in sunlight, worn set pieces mend 1 durability every 30 seconds.

The **Rootstaff** does 7 damage with a little more reach (siphon 2).

## Maldritch's Remains {#maldritch}

```spotlight magictreasures:bone_scythe
The apprentice who became a lich: bone, and stolen life. Maldritch's First Amulet, Ring of Life and Death, Skull Belt, Bone Scythe and Lich's Mask.
```

- **2:** Drain spells reach 1 block further.
- **4:** half the life your Drain spells take comes back as mana.
- **5, Bound Possessions:** if you die wearing the full set, its worn pieces stay with you and are back in their slots when you respawn.

The **Bone Scythe** does 9 damage with more reach, and siphons the most (3). See also the [Skull Belt](entry:skull_belt).

## Selene's Tides {#selene}

```spotlight magictreasures:moonglaive
The sorceress of pearls and the sea. Aqua Ring, Journeyman's Bands, Eye of the Phoenix, Moonglaive and Pearl Circlet.
```

- **2:** Water Breathing and Night Vision last 50% longer.
- **4, Tidewalker:** in water you swim with Dolphin's Grace, and the set's jewelry regains 1 mana every 5 seconds.
- **5, Moonlit:** every spell costs 25% less at night (never in the Nether or the End).

The **Moonglaive** does 8 damage with more reach (siphon 2).
