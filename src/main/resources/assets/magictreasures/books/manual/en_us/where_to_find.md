## Ores {#ores}

Gem and silver ores generate in the Overworld, in stone and deepslate, most often around the middle of their range.

- **Topaz, Onyx:** Y -30 to 30 (most at 0). Iron pickaxe.
- **Silver:** Y -30 to 26 (most at -2). Stone pickaxe.
- **Jadeite:** Y -40 to 20 (most at -10). Iron pickaxe.
- **Ruby, Sapphire:** Y -50 to 6 (most at -22). Iron pickaxe.

Gem ores drop their gem, and Fortune gives more. Silver Ore drops Raw Silver. Pearls and diamonds don't come from Magic Treasures ores.

## Chests {#chests}

When a chest rolls Magic Treasures loot, it adds jewelry, gems and spell scrolls of the chest's **rarity**:

- **Uncommon:** village houses and workshops.
- **Scarce:** abandoned mineshafts.
- **Rare:** bastions, buried treasure, nether fortresses, pillager outposts, dungeons, small underwater ruins.
- **Epic:** mineshafts, ancient cities, desert pyramids, igloos, jungle temples, shipwreck treasure, big underwater ruins.
- **Legendary:** bastion treasure, stronghold libraries and crossings.
- **Mythical:** end cities, woodland mansions.

From Scarce up, recipe scrolls can turn up too, and from Rare up so can the [Skull Belt](entry:skull_belt). Chests also hold [lore books](entry:lore_books) and [set](entry:sets) weapons and armor.

## Mobs {#mobs}

Many mobs can drop gems, spell scrolls, jewelry and recipe scrolls; tougher mobs drop better jewelry and Recharge Scrolls. Looting raises the chances.

- **Zombies, husks, drowned:** wood rings, gems, Harm scrolls.
- **Skeletons, strays, wither skeletons:** bone jewelry, gems, spell scrolls.
- **Blazes, ghasts, guardians, shulkers, vindicators and more:** jewelry up to Rare.
- **Elder guardian, ender dragon, ravager, warden, wither:** Rare to Mythical jewelry.

## Treasure2 {#treasure2}

With Treasure2 installed, its treasure chests are filled with Magic Treasures loot matched to their rarity: the best way to find high-level jewelry and spells.
