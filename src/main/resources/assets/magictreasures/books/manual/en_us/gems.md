Set a gem in jewelry at an anvil: the jewelry in the first slot, the gem in the second.

A gem adds mana and recharges, and the better gems also make spells cheaper, faster and stronger.

Treasure2's gems count the same as these. Modpacks can add their own gems.

## Removing a Gem {#remove_gem}

Two ways, and you choose which to keep:


- **Shears** at an anvil take the gem out and keep the jewelry. The gem is lost.
- **Jewelry Pliers**, crafted with the jewelry, give the gem back. The jewelry is lost.
