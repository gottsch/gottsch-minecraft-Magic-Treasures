## Recipe Scrolls {#recipe_scrolls}

```spotlight magictreasures:ring_recipe
```

There are three: **Ring Recipe**, **Necklace Recipe** and **Bracelet Recipe**. Find them in chests and mob drops.

Combine a scroll with **4 of a material** anywhere in a crafting table. The scroll is used up.

- **Iron:** 4 Iron Ingots
- **Copper:** 4 Copper Ingots
- **Silver:** 4 Silver Ingots
- **Gold:** 4 Gold Ingots
- **Bone:** 4 Bones

Crafted jewelry is Regular style, with no gem and no spells. Next, [add a gem and a spell](entry:anvil). Wood, Great and Lord's jewelry, and jewelry with a gem already set, are only found as loot.

The ingredients use common tags, so ingots and bones from other mods work too.

## Jewelry Pliers {#pliers}

```recipe magictreasures:jewelry_pliers
```

Put the pliers and a piece of jewelry with a gem in a crafting grid to **get the gem back**. {dark_red}The jewelry is destroyed{}, along with its spells. The pliers come back with 1 use spent; they last 64 uses.

This is the opposite of [removing a gem at the anvil](entry:anvil#remove_gem), which keeps the jewelry and destroys the gem.

## Silver {#silver}

```spotlight magictreasures:silver_ingot
```

Silver is only used for jewelry. Mine **Silver Ore** (stone pickaxe or better) for Raw Silver, then smelt it into Silver Ingots. See [Where to Find](entry:where_to_find#ores).
