---
title: Letters Across the Valley, II
author: Sal'andaar and Silbro
---

Friend,

The boy's mother is buried. I sat with her three days and could not keep her. He thanked me at the grave, politely, and did not look at me once.

Watch him.

\- Silbro

---

Silbro,

He's back at the anvil. Works harder than before. Best hands I've had.

Grief is grief. Leave him be.

\- S. Stone

---

A fox came to my door, alive and hollow as a scraped gourd. Something drew it empty and let it walk away.

I have only met one person who asked me how a thing could be emptied.

\- Silbro

---

Silbro,

Foxes get sick.

The boy has no one but this forge. I won't turn him out on a druid's feeling.

\- S. Stone

---

They say in the market that the mill village on the east road stands empty. They say you made the reeve a ward for his door, and would not take his coin.

Write to me, Stone.

\- Silbro

---

Friend,

A year, and no letter. The runner says the forge is lit, so I know you are alive, and that is all I know.

I will not ask what you knew. Only write.

\- Silbro
